Common questions

Is ad spend included?

No. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta from your own account and is never marked up. Our fee covers the website, editing, posting, campaign management and reporting.

Do I have to film?

Yes, but it is quick. We send you a simple shot list and you film short clips on your phone while you work. We do everything after that.

I don't have a website or social media.

That is fine, and common. We build the website first and set up the accounts as part of the work.

Can you guarantee jobs?

No one honestly can. What we promise is the work we control, plus the next month free if you do your part for 60 days and enquiries still are not coming through.

Can I start below £3,000?

Our starting plan is £3,000 a month. If that is not right for your business yet, we will tell you honestly.

What is the contract length?

3 months minimum, then month to month.
