Options

Ways we can work together

Fixed monthly fee

A set monthly fee agreed from your revenue, reviewed as you grow.

Share of results

A share of the revenue or profit we help you generate. Ideal when cash is tight early on.

A mix of both

A smaller fixed fee plus a share, so we are rewarded when you win.

Common questions

Is ad spend included?

No. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta from your own account and is never marked up. Our fee covers strategy, creative, editing and management.

Can I start small?

Yes. Many of the businesses we work with are early stage. Tell us what you can comfortably invest and we will build around it.

How do you decide the fee?

From your application and a short call. We look at where your business is, its revenue and profit, and your goals, then propose terms that make sense for both sides.

Do I need to share my numbers?

Rough ranges on the form are enough. We go through detail on the call, in confidence.

What is the contract length?

A short initial term agreed in writing, then month to month.
