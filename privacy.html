Privacy

How we use your details.

SOLO Agency is run by Tommaso Cristoni, London, UK. This note explains what we do with the details you send through this website. It is a plain-language summary, not legal advice.

What we collect

When you apply or contact us: your name, email, phone number, business or brand name, website or social handles, and your answers about your business (such as stage, revenue and profit ranges, what you need, and your goals).

Why

To understand where you are, reply to you, and decide whether and how we can work together. We do not sell your details and do not use them for unrelated marketing.

How it is handled

Form submissions are sent to us by email through a form service (FormSubmit). We keep applications only as long as needed to reply and to run any work that follows, and delete them on request.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time by emailing solo.studios.co@gmail.com.
